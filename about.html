About Rojetz

Lucena's Favorite Ihaw-ihaw

Our Story

Rojetz BBQ Station started with a simple passion: to serve the best tasting, most affordable, and consistently delicious grilled food in Lucena City.

Known as "Lucena's Favorite Ihaw-ihaw", we take pride in our slogan "Sarap Sulit... Mapapaulit!" — because once you try our BBQ and isaw, you'll keep coming back.

We also offer Simply Siomai, SulitKasalo sharing meals, fried chicken, Specialty Blends and drip coffee. Whether you're grabbing a quick merienda or ordering a full meal via Foodpanda, we're here to satisfy your cravings.

Why Choose Us?

  • Freshly grilled every order
  • Affordable & value-for-money
  • Friendly service (even late night!)
  • Available on Foodpanda
  • Also found at Mercato Lucena night market
