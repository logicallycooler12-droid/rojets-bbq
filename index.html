Our Story

From Simple Passion to Lucena's Favorite

Rojetz BBQ Station started with one goal — to serve the best tasting, most affordable, and consistently delicious grilled food in Lucena City.

What began as a small passion project has grown into a local favorite known for its slogan: "Sarap Sulit... Mapapaulit!"
